2. Data the App handles

  • Your library: song titles, authors, notes, tempo and rhythm settings, tags, setlists and their ordering, arrangement sections and cue text.
  • Practice history: practice start times and durations, song-title snapshots, tempos, rhythm settings, section names and associated tags.
  • Settings and sync records: preferences, purchase entitlement status, record identifiers, modification times, deletion markers, pending sync uploads and last-sync information.
  • Google profile, when you sign in: your email address, Google account identifier, display name and profile photo. These identify the connected account, display your profile and keep sync records associated with the correct account. The App obtains an OAuth access token to communicate with Google Drive.
  • Purchase information: Google Play supplies purchase status, product identifiers and purchase tokens so the App can acknowledge purchases and unlock or restore premium features. The App does not receive your card number or payment credentials.
  • Support correspondence: if you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostics you choose to send. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

The App has no advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting service. Practice statistics are calculated from your practice history; they are not an advertising or tracking feature.

3. Optional Google sign-in and Drive sync

You can use the App’s local features without connecting a Google account. Cloud sync is a premium feature that requires Google sign-in, the relevant Google permissions, an internet connection and sufficient Google Drive storage. Once connected and enabled, sync can run automatically as well as when you request it.

The App requests the drive.appdata permission to read and write its own hidden application data folder. This permission does not grant general access to your other Drive documents. Sync files are separate from your ordinary Drive files and from Android device backups.

Sync uploads the library, arrangement sections, practice history and synchronization records described above directly from your device to Google Drive, and downloads and merges them on your connected devices. These are data records, not audio recordings or uploaded music files. The Developer does not receive a copy through a separate backend.

Google provides sign-in and cloud storage and handles data under its Privacy Policy. We use Google data only to provide account identification and the App’s visible sync features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Metrum’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including their Limited Use requirements.

6. Storage and security

Your library, history and preferences are stored in the App’s private storage on your device. Drive communication uses HTTPS and authorized Google access tokens. Sync files are JSON data; the App does not add end-to-end encryption or a separate password to them. Google controls the security of its services, and your device and account security also matter.

The App permits Android system backup. Depending on your Android version, device settings and backup provider, local App data may be included in a cloud backup or device transfer independently of the App’s Drive sync setting. Manage those copies through your device’s backup settings and provider.

No storage or transmission system can guarantee complete security. Protect your device and Google account and contact us if you suspect a problem.

7. Retention and what item deletion means

Local library and practice records remain in App storage until that storage is cleared. Deleting a song or clearing practice history marks records as deleted so that the deletion can sync between devices; it does not immediately erase the record’s stored fields.

Drive sync retains historical snapshots and deletion records indefinitely until the App’s hidden Drive data is removed. Earlier snapshots can contain the content of items you have since deleted. The current App does not automatically expire those snapshots or offer permanent erasure of individual records across all stored copies.

Signing out, disabling sync or uninstalling the Android App does not itself delete the hidden Drive sync data. Signing out also does not itself revoke Google permissions. Previously accepted uploads may remain even if a sync operation is stopped.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry and related follow-up, or to meet an applicable legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Google, GitHub and your backup provider apply their own retention rules to records they control.

8. Stop sync or permanently remove your data

Deleting an item in the App is different from erasing stored copies. To remove the entire library and history from local and cloud storage, follow all the relevant steps below. This cannot be undone.

  1. Stop sync on every connected device. Disable cloud sync in Settings or sign out by tapping your Google profile in the navigation drawer. Do this before deleting cloud data to prevent another device from uploading it again.
  2. Remove the cloud copies. Open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Metrum, and use its Options menu to delete hidden app data. See Google’s guidance on hidden app data. This removes the App’s cloud library, history and historical sync files, rather than your unrelated Drive documents.
  3. Revoke access if desired. Remove the App’s access in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it is not a substitute for deleting stored copies.
  4. Clear local copies on every device. In Android Settings, open Apps → Metrum → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage/data. Menu names vary by device. This removes your local library, history and App settings.
  5. Check device backups. Remove any remaining Android backups using your backup provider’s controls, and avoid restoring a backup containing the old App data. Reconnecting sync from a device or restored backup that still contains your data can upload it again.

The App does not create a separate Developer-hosted account. Your Google account belongs to you and is not deleted by these steps. We cannot remotely erase data from your device or access your Drive to delete it for you. If you need help, or want to request deletion of support correspondence held by us, contact metrum.metronome.contact@gmail.com.
